# UI audit of the live site, 2026-11-14 (for the design team)

What this is: a walk through the live product at https://lunchvoice.com in a real, signed-in Chrome window (desktop, a very wide
display) plus headless Chrome at 390 and 768 px for the public pages. Everything below was **seen on the live site today** (label F)
unless marked J (judgment) or A (to confirm). Nothing was changed, submitted or deleted. Defect IDs are used by `UPGRADE_LEDGER.md`
and by the week-4 acceptance. Severity: **P0** blocks launch, **P1** visibly unfinished or inconsistent, **P2** polish.

Captured screens at 390 and 768 px are in `docs/frontend/design-research/captures/` (gitignored, local): `home`, `pricing`,
`templates`, `discover`, `waitlist`, `how`, `kitchen`, `signin`, each `-390.png` and `-768.png`.

## 0. What was not tested (be honest about the gaps)

Dark and high-contrast themes; keyboard-only use; the signed-in screens at phone and tablet width (the Chrome window could not be
made narrower than the display, and the DevTools emulator failed to start); Lighthouse; the admin console; elections, Present mode
and live results; the sign-in flow itself; console errors; real-device Safari. The design team fills these in week 1 (`UPGRADE_LEDGER.md`).

## 1. One-page summary

| Theme | What is wrong | Worst examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **No system for page width** | Every page picks its own column (about 530, 750, 800 or 1,180 px) and floats it in the middle of a wide screen, so the app uses roughly a third of a large monitor | Results 530 px; Hub pages 560 to 750 px; survey editor mixes 800 and 1,180 px |
| **Navigation is inconsistent** | The top tabs (Home, Surveys, Join, Settings) exist on some pages and not on others; Hubs, Templates, Trends and Billing are not reachable from them | No tabs on Trends, Reserve, Developers, every Hub page |
| **The app looks like a form, not a product** | Stacked white cards, small grey text, grey-filled inputs, no imagery, no data visuals in empty states | Survey editor, My home, Hub overview |
| **Components touch or wrap** | Stat tiles stacked edge to edge; header buttons wrapping onto a second row | `/me`, Hub overview, survey editor header |
| **The respondent player is plain** | It should be the liveliest screen and is the plainest | Player choices, grey Next button |
| **Unfinished or stale copy is public** | "Coming soon" on shipped features; "Status reporting isn't wired up yet" | Pricing, `/status` |
| **A privacy defect** | A member's email address is shown as their name | Hub Members and Overview |

## 3. What already works (keep)

The identity (amber, ink, bone; Bricolage headlines) is distinctive; the landing copy is clear and honest; the "withheld, never zero"
results language; the template preview ("Try it") with a live question; the Hub colour and avatar; the sign-in screen at phone width;
the template picker structure on New Quick Survey; no horizontal overflow on any public page checked.

## 4. Changes to the plan from this audit

1. **HB1 is a launch blocker** (privacy): added to spec E1 as a rule and to the security list; the Backend agent fixes the name fallback in
   week 1 (it is a display-name rule in the API, not a UI skin), and a test proves no email appears in any member list, Hub card, comment or
   leaderboard.
2. **PL2 and P1 are P0** for week 4: the player and the pricing copy.
3. The shared patterns (page header, stat grid, forms, container widths, one app shell) are built first in week 4 because G1, G2, G4, G6 and
   E1 recur on most routes.
4. `UPGRADE_LEDGER.md` rows now carry these defect IDs; the design team adds the not-tested items (section 0) in week 1.
