# LunchVoice user guide

For people who use LunchVoice: answering, asking, and running a Hub. It describes the product as it is live on
2026-11-10. This file is the source for a future help centre at `/support`; each `##` section is one article.

Words used here: a **survey** is anything you ask (inside a Hub it is also called a Campaign). A **Hub** is a
community's home at `lunchvoice.com/its-name`. A **code** is the six characters that open a survey.

---

## Answering a survey

### How do I answer?

Open the link you were sent, scan the QR code, or go to `lunchvoice.com/join` and type the six-character code. You
will see the survey's title and a short note about privacy, then one question per screen. Press **Submit** on the
last one.

You do not need an account or an app unless the survey is for members of a Hub or is invite-only. In that case you
will be asked to sign in first.

### Is it really anonymous?

It depends on the survey, and the first screen always tells you which kind it is before you answer.

- **Anonymous:** the person who made the survey sees totals only. They cannot find out who answered, and LunchVoice
  does not show them when each answer arrived. Nothing is shown while the survey is open, and if fewer than 5
  people answered, results stay withheld.
  If only a few people chose an option, that option is folded into a "withheld" group so nobody can be picked out.
  Written answers are shown as a shuffled list with no names and no times, and only once 5 or more exist.
- **Named:** the creator sees your answers with your name. If you are answering as a guest, the survey may ask you
  to type a name; it is shown to the creator as "Guest, unverified".

A tip for written answers on anonymous surveys: do not include details that identify you. LunchVoice cannot hide
what you type yourself.

### Can I change my answer?

No. Each person answers a survey once. If you close the page before submitting, your progress is kept on that device
so you can finish later.

### It says the survey is closed, scheduled or unavailable

- **Closed:** it has ended and takes no more answers.
- **Scheduled:** it has not opened yet. The page shows when it opens.
- **Unavailable:** it is for members of a Hub, or it has been paused or removed.
- **"Too many responses":** a safety limit was reached, which should be rare. Wait a minute and press Submit
  again; your answers are still on the page.

### I think a survey is abusive or a scam

Use **Report this survey** on the survey page. Reports go to LunchVoice staff, not to the survey's creator.

## Your account

### Do I need one?

Only to make surveys, join a private Hub, or answer an invite-only or members-only survey. Answering open surveys
never needs one. Accounts are free.

### How do I sign in?

Choose **Sign in** and continue with Google. You pass through `auth.kpnsolute.com`: that is KpnIdentity, the sign-in
service of KpnSolute, the company that makes LunchVoice. LunchVoice never sees your Google password.

For security a session lasts one hour. If you are asked to sign in again, do so and carry on.

### How do I delete my account?

**Account settings → Delete account**, then type `DELETE`. This removes your profile, sign-in link, memberships,
invites and settings. Your written answers are deleted. Your other answers stay only as part of totals, with the
link to you permanently removed. If you own a Hub, transfer or delete it first.

### Light, dark and high contrast

Use the theme control in the account menu. LunchVoice follows your device setting unless you choose otherwise.

## Asking: free Quick Surveys

### What do I get for free?

Up to **5 active surveys** at a time, each with up to **10 questions**. Share by link, QR code or six-character
code, or keep it invite-only. Basic totals and charts. Closing or archiving a survey frees its slot.

### Make your first survey

1. Sign in and choose **New survey**.
2. Give it a title that tells people what it is about.
3. Add questions. There are five kinds:

   | Kind | Use it for |
   | --- | --- |
   | Rating | "How was it?" on a scale |
   | Single choice | Pick one |
   | Multiple choice | Pick any that apply |
   | Yes / No | A quick decision |
   | Text | Anything in their own words |

4. Choose **who can answer**: anyone with the link, or only people you invite (they must sign in).
5. Choose **Anonymous** or **Named**. This cannot be changed once the first answer arrives, because people answered
   under that promise.
6. Decide whether **guests** (people without an account) can answer. For a named survey you can ask guests to type
   a name, optionally or as a requirement.
7. Optionally set when it opens and closes.
8. Press **Publish**. The readiness list beside the button tells you if anything is missing.

### Share it

After publishing you get a link, a six-character code and a QR code. Put the QR on a slide or a poster, paste the
link in a chat, or read the code aloud: people type it at `lunchvoice.com/join`.

### Read the results

Open the survey and choose **Results**.

- An **anonymous** survey is sealed while it is open: no results and no counts. This is deliberate. If you could
  watch results change as each person answered, you could work out who said what. Results appear when the survey
  closes, and only if at least 5 people answered.
- A **named** survey shows results as answers come in and refreshes on its own.
- **Withheld** means a number is hidden because showing it could single someone out. It is never the same as zero.
  When any part of a question is withheld, its average is withheld too.
- Results state how many answers came from guests, because guests are not verified.
- For a **named** survey a **Responses** list shows each person's answers.

### Pause, edit, close

- **Pause** stops answers without ending the survey. While paused you can always edit the title, description and
  schedule. You can edit the questions and the anonymous / named choice only until the first answer arrives.
- **Close** ends it. Results stay.
- **Archive** puts it away. Nothing is deleted.

### Tips for more and better answers

- Three questions get far more answers than ten. Ask only what you will act on.
- Say why you are asking, in the description.
- Ask while people are together: end of a meeting, a pinned message, a slide.
- Afterwards, tell people what you learned and what you will do. It is the best way to get answers next time.

## Hubs

### What is a Hub?

A home for one community at `lunchvoice.com/your-name`: members, roles, pages and all its surveys in one place.
Choose a Hub when the same group will be asked more than once, or when only certain people should be able to answer.

### Create a Hub

You need a plan that includes a Hub (see Plans). Choose **Create a Hub**, pick a name and an address, and choose:

- **Visibility:** public (anyone can see the Hub page) or private.
- **How people join:** open, by request with your approval, or by invite only.

You become the **Owner**. Some addresses are reserved by LunchVoice and cannot be chosen.

### Members and invites

- **Invites:** create an invite link or code, optionally with a limit on uses and an expiry date. Revoke it anytime.
- **Requests:** if joining needs approval, pending people appear under **Members**; admit or decline them.
- **Remove** a member anytime. Members can also leave on their own.
- Guests can answer open surveys in a Hub but are never members, never count toward member totals, and cannot see
  private pages.

### Roles

Roles decide what a member may do: manage members, build surveys, read results, edit pages, see billing, and so on.

- The **Owner** can do everything, cannot be removed, and does not use up a role slot.
- You can create **3** custom roles on the Hub plan and **6** on Team.
- Give people only what they need. Reading results is its own permission.
- To hand over the Hub, use **Transfer ownership**; the other person must accept.

### Surveys in a Hub (Campaigns)

Inside a Hub a survey is called a Campaign and has more options than a Quick Survey:

- **Audience:** all members, or on Team, only members with certain roles.
- **Members only** or also open to guests by link.
- **Schedule:** Draft → Scheduled → Open → Paused → Closed → Archived.
- A Hub has limits on how many surveys can be active at once, depending on its plan. The Hub's **Billing** page
  shows what you are using.

### Pages and appearance

- **Appearance:** choose your Hub's color from a set that keeps text readable, and add a short description.
- **Pages:** add your own pages (3 on the Hub plan, more on Team) and turn sections on or off. **News** lets you post
  updates, including what you did about feedback.

### Discord

Add the LunchVoice bot to your server to post a survey to a channel with an **Answer** button.

- `/lunchvoice connect` (run by a server admin) links a server to your Hub.
- `/lunchvoice post` posts a survey.
- `/lunchvoice link` lets a member connect their Discord and LunchVoice accounts; `/lunchvoice unlink` undoes it.
- Anonymous surveys are always answered on LunchVoice, never inside Discord, because Discord can see who clicks
  buttons there.

### Delete a Hub

**Hub settings → Delete Hub**, then type the Hub's address to confirm. This deletes the Hub and its data and cannot
be undone. The address is held for 30 days before anyone can claim it.

## Privacy in plain words

- Anonymous means the creator cannot find out who answered. LunchVoice enforces this in its database, not just on
  the screen.
- Small groups are withheld. A withheld number is never shown as zero.
- Guests do not need an account. To stop double answers, your browser keeps a random key; LunchVoice stores only a
  scrambled form of it and removes the link after 7 days.
- Survey data is kept while the account or Hub that owns it exists. Security logs are kept for 12 months.
- LunchVoice does not sell data and sends no marketing email.

The full text is at `lunchvoice.com/privacy`, and the community rules are at `lunchvoice.com/guidelines`.

## Getting help

Write to **admin@lunchvoice.com**. Say what you were doing and include the survey code or Hub address if there is
one. Never send passwords.
