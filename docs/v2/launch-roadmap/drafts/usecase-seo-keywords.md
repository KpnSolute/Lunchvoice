# Use-case pages: SEO keyword plan (draft, M3)

Status: planned. Nothing is live. These are the ten existing pages at `/use-cases/<slug>` (source: `src/config/useCases.json`).
Same rules as `audience-seo-keywords.md`: one main keyword per page in the title, H1 and first paragraph; the other 49 or
more appear naturally in headings, the FAQ, sample questions and template text; no `meta keywords` tag; no stuffing; every
claim is something the product does.

**Avoid pages competing with each other.** Each page's *main* keyword is unique across the whole site (audience pages
included). Secondary terms may overlap, but the page that is the best answer owns the term and the others link to it.
