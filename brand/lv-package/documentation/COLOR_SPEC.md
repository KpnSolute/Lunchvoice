# LunchVoice colour specification

Generated by `npm run brand:build` from `src/ui/tokens.css`, which is the source of truth. Do not edit this file by hand, and do not introduce a colour that is not in it.

Contrast ratios are WCAG 2 ratios, rounded down. Text needs 4.5:1 (3:1 for text of 24px and up, or 19px bold and up, and for icons and control outlines).

## Brand colours

| Name | HEX | RGB | HSL | Put on it | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Amber | `#FF9A18` | 255, 154, 24 | 34, 100%, 55% | Ink | Accent. Fills, the primary button, the logo's bubble and smile. |
| Ink | `#14110F` | 20, 17, 15 | 24, 14%, 7% | Bone or white | Anchor. Text, the logo's letters, dark bands. |
| Bone | `#F7F3EA` | 247, 243, 234 | 42, 45%, 94% | Ink | Text and logo letters on ink; a warm light background in marketing. |
| Page | `#EEEDEA` | 238, 237, 234 | 45, 11%, 93% | Ink | The neutral page background. |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | 255, 255, 255 | 0, 0%, 100% | Ink | Cards and surfaces; the dots in the logo's bubble. |

How they work together: ink anchors the identity, bone and the neutral page keep it calm, and amber is the accent. On any one screen or graphic, amber marks the single most important thing. It is never used for ordinary text on a light background: on white it measures 2.1:1.

What this means in practice:

- Amber on a light background fails for text and for thin icons. Use Amber text (`--lv-accent-text`) when amber has to be read, or put the amber on ink.
- White text on amber fails. Text on amber is always ink.
- The logo's amber bubble and smile are large filled shapes, not text, and are paired with ink or bone letters that carry the contrast.

## Charts and data

The product has no separate chart palette. Charts use ink (or bone in the dark theme) for bars, amber for the highlighted value, the well colour for tracks, and a striped ink and well pattern, never a colour alone, for a withheld result. A second data series should be told apart by pattern or label before a new colour is proposed.

## Warning

There is no warning colour token. Warning notices use the accent (amber) with ink text. Inside a Hub the accent becomes the Hub's own colour, so a warning there takes the Hub's colour: a known issue recorded for a founder decision, not something to patch with a new colour.

## Changing a colour

A new colour, or a new value for an existing one, needs founder approval (`docs/frontend/design-system/DESIGN_EXTENSIONS.md`). Nothing in this kit changed a value.
