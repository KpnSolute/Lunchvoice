# LunchVoice colours for Canva and other tools

| Name | HEX | RGB | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Amber | `#FF9A18` | 255, 154, 24 | The accent. Buttons, highlights, the logo's bubble and smile |
| Ink | `#14110F` | 20, 17, 15 | Text, dark backgrounds |
| Bone | `#F7F3EA` | 247, 243, 234 | Text on ink; a warm light background |
| Page | `#EEEDEA` | 238, 237, 234 | Neutral light background |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | 255, 255, 255 | Cards |
| Amber text | `#8F4A00` | 143, 74, 0 | Amber-coloured words on a light background |
| Muted | `#4A4540` | 74, 69, 64 | Supporting text on light |
| Soft text on ink | `#C9C3BA` | 201, 195, 186 | Supporting text on ink |

Rules:

- One amber thing per design.
- Never amber words on a light background; use Amber text or switch the background to ink.
- Words on amber are ink, not white.
- A design is either light (ink on Page, Bone or White) or dark (Bone on Ink).

The full specification with contrast measurements is `../documentation/COLOR_SPEC.md`.
