## Recommended release rhythm
- Start with V01; review its official logo and message on a phone.
- Release V02 and V03 after validating with real product footage.
- Release V04 only after rechecking current Hub capabilities and purchase/entitlement status.
- Release V05 once a working real-screen demo is captured and edited.
- Recut longer footage into Shorts when doing so keeps all claims accurate.

## Source truth / checkpoints
- `DESIGN.md`, `src/ui/tokens.css` and `brand/lv-package/README.txt`: approved colors, type and logo.
- `docs/frontend/GO_TO_MARKET.md`: approved audience, positioning, offer and channels.
- `docs/v2/LAUNCH_PLAN.md`: marketing restrictions and prelaunch waitlist decisions.
- `VIDEO_PROMPTS.md`: ready-to-paste prompts for V01–V05.

## Publication checklist (repeat for every video)
- [ ] Uses genuine repository logo and brand colors
- [ ] No invented UI, statistics, testimonials, partnerships or unsupported promises
- [ ] Accurate offer and CTA for the actual public site
- [ ] Age-appropriate marketing to adults, not minors
- [ ] Correct 9:16 (Reels/Shorts) or 16:9 (YouTube) format, text within safe areas
- [ ] Readable on mobile, captions checked, accessible contrast
- [ ] Commercial rights of all stock/audio/video verified; watermark acceptable or removed legally
- [ ] Final edit reviewed and approved
- [ ] Published URL, date and outcomes added to production record
